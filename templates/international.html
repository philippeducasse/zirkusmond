Zirkus Mond around the world

Zirkus Mond International is a circus network with locations in Berlin (Germany), Lisbon (Portugal) and Guadalajara (Mexico) that promotes circus arts and the creative industry. Through cooperation and the exchange of knowledge and experience it seeks to connect communities through art and thus promote cultural exchange. Zirkus Mond was born in Berlin in 2018 as an independent circus project with a permanent circus tent in the city centre dedicated to circus, dance and other performing arts. From its beginnings and through a varied and dynamic programme of shows and activities, the aim is to promote and encourage circus arts and boost the circus industry. To this end, it is committed to giving opportunities to artists with innovative proposals and emerging companies. It is a space for creation and experimentation that serves as a launching pad to boost their professional careers. The essence of this cultural exchange has driven the expansion of the project to new latitudes, building an international circus network.
